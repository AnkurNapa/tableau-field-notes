Why this site exists

I spent more than eight years as an R&D master brewer before I touched a dashboard. The brewhouse taught me that a gravity reading means nothing until you know when the sample was pulled and who pulled it. Tableau taught me the same thing about a SUM.

Most Tableau learning is either a feature tour or a gallery of beautiful vizzes. Very little of it tells a beginner what to learn next, or why the FIXED calculation they copied from a forum gives a different number once a filter goes on. This site is a roadmap that does, kept honest by the people I learn with every month in the user groups.

How the notes are written

  • Every walkthrough carries a Tableau as of stamp, so you know which release it describes.
  • Product facts are checked against Tableau Help. When the documentation and my screen disagree, I say so and show both.
  • Examples use synthetic data, mostly from breweries, distilleries, wineries and a made-up retailer. Nothing comes from anyone's real business.
  • When I get something wrong, I correct it in public.
