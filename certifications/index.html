Road to Tableau certification

Six exams, all run by Salesforce through Trailhead Academy since Tableau certification moved there. A certificate will not build your first dashboard for you. It does make you learn the corners you would otherwise skip, like the order of operations.

FoundationsData AnalystServer AdministratorConsultantArchitectTableau Next Consultant

The ladder

  1. 1

    Analytics-101

    Salesforce Certified Tableau Desktop Foundations

    The entry exam. Proves you can build and read a worksheet in Tableau Desktop.

    Who it is for
    People with foundational Tableau Desktop skills and at least three months of using them.
    Prerequisites
    None.
    Format
    40 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions in 70 minutes.
    Cost
    USD 75 plus local taxes. Retakes are free.
    Validity and renewal
    Not in the Winter 26 Tableau maintenance module, which covers the other four exams. Check your Trailhead profile for its status.

    As of October 2026. Source: exam page on Trailhead Academy.

    Roadmap steps that prepare you

    The Tableau family in one pictureBlue pills and green pillsShelves, the Marks card and Show MeYour first workbookDates done rightCalculated fieldsQuick table calculationsMaps with geographic rolesYour first dashboard

    Study order

    1. Learn the pill colours and the shelves
    2. Build ten small worksheets from one CSV
    3. Dates, calculated fields and quick table calcs
    4. Maps and a first dashboard
    5. Sit a timed practice run before you book
  2. 2

    Analytics-DA-201

    Salesforce Certified Tableau Data Analyst

    The working analyst exam across Desktop, Prep and Server or Cloud.

    Who it is for
    Analysts who frame the business question, find the data and deliver the answer with Tableau Desktop, Tableau Prep and Tableau Server or Cloud.
    Prerequisites
    None.
    Format
    60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions plus five unscored ones, in 105 minutes.
    Cost
    USD 200 plus local taxes. Retake USD 100.
    Validity and renewal
    Annual maintenance module on Trailhead. Miss the due date and the certification expires.

    As of October 2026. Source: exam page on Trailhead Academy.

    Roadmap steps that prepare you

    Why Prep existsCalculated fieldsYour first dashboardYour first Prep flowPublish and shareLOD expressionsTable calcs: addressing and partitioningThe order of operationsParameters and parameter actionsSets and set actionsCharts beyond Show MeRelationships and multi-fact modelsPivots, aggregates and calculations in PrepSchedule flows with Prep ConductorRow-level securityPublished data sources and refresh schedules

    Study order

    1. LODs until FIXED against a context filter stops surprising you
    2. Table calc addressing and partitioning
    3. The order of operations, drawn out by hand once
    4. Prep flows: clean, pivot, join and output
    5. Publishing, permissions and refresh schedules
  3. 3

    Analytics-Admn-201

    Salesforce Certified Tableau Server Administrator

    Installing, securing and running a single-node Tableau Server.

    Who it is for
    Administrators who know Tableau Server functionality on a single machine, with about six months of experience.
    Prerequisites
    None.
    Format
    55 multiple-choice and multiple-select items in 90 minutes.
    Cost
    USD 200 plus local taxes. Retake USD 100.
    Validity and renewal
    Annual maintenance module on Trailhead. Miss the due date and the certification expires.

    As of October 2026. Source: exam page on Trailhead Academy.

    Roadmap steps that prepare you

    Server, Cloud and the three licencesPublish and shareSchedule flows with Prep ConductorPublished data sources and refresh schedulesSites, projects and the permission modelMonitor usage and healthExtracts and backgrounder load

    Study order

    1. Install a trial Server on a spare machine and break it
    2. Sites, projects, groups and the permission model
    3. Extract schedules and backgrounder load
    4. TSM, backups and restores
    5. Monitoring with the admin views
  4. 4

    Analytics-Con-301

    Salesforce Certified Tableau Consultant

    Design, performance, scale, adoption and governance, judged across the whole stack.

    Who it is for
    Consultants who help customers get value from their data, working across Tableau Prep, Desktop, Cloud, Server and Bridge.
    Prerequisites
    None.
    Format
    60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions plus up to five unscored ones, in 105 minutes.
    Cost
    USD 200 plus local taxes. Retake USD 100.
    Validity and renewal
    Annual maintenance module on Trailhead. Miss the due date and the certification expires.

    As of October 2026. Source: exam page on Trailhead Academy.

    Roadmap steps that prepare you

    Row-level securityFix a slow workbookSites, projects and the permission modelIdentity, SSO and private dataRunning a centre of excellenceOne KPI, one definition

    Study order

    1. Pass Data Analyst first, or study to that level
    2. Performance Recorder on a slow workbook of your own
    3. Row-level security patterns and when each one fails
    4. Tableau Blueprint for governance and adoption
    5. Practise explaining a trade-off to a non-technical sponsor
  5. 5

    Analytics-Arch-201

    Salesforce Certified Tableau Architect

    Designing, deploying and keeping a scalable Tableau platform healthy.

    Who it is for
    Architects who design, deploy, monitor and maintain Tableau Cloud and Tableau Server with customers.
    Prerequisites
    None.
    Format
    59 multiple-choice and multiple-select items in 105 minutes.
    Cost
    USD 400 plus local taxes. Retake USD 200.
    Validity and renewal
    Annual maintenance module on Trailhead. Miss the due date and the certification expires.

    As of October 2026. Source: exam page on Trailhead Academy.

    Roadmap steps that prepare you

    Extracts and backgrounder loadCloud or Server: the deployment decisionServer topology and scalingIdentity, SSO and private dataMigrate Server to CloudRunning a centre of excellenceGoverned data at scaleEmbedded analytics as a productAgentic analytics architecture

    Study order

    1. Server Administrator material, cold
    2. Cloud or Server: write the decision table
    3. Topology, node sizing and process placement
    4. Identity: SAML, OIDC, SCIM and Bridge
    5. Migration planning and the Content Migration Tool

Off the main ladder: Tableau Next

Tableau Next lives inside Salesforce, so its exam leans on Data Cloud more than on Desktop. Take it when your work is on Tableau Next, not as the next rung.

  1. N

    Analytics-Con-202

    Salesforce Certified Tableau Next Consultant

    The newest exam. Tableau Next, semantic models and Data Cloud inside Salesforce.

    Who it is for
    Practitioners who implement, configure and maintain Tableau Next solutions in the Salesforce ecosystem, using Data Cloud and Semantic Data Models.
    Prerequisites
    None.
    Format
    60 multiple-choice questions plus up to five unscored ones, in 105 minutes.
    Cost
    USD 200 plus local taxes. Retake USD 100.
    Validity and renewal
    Launched in August 2026. Expect the usual Salesforce maintenance cycle; check the exam page for the first due date.

    As of October 2026. Source: exam page on Trailhead Academy.

    Roadmap steps that prepare you

    Agentic analytics architecture

    Study order

    1. Data Cloud basics: streams, data model objects and identity
    2. Semantic Data Models and metric definitions
    3. Pulse metrics and the agent experience
    4. Governance and security in Salesforce terms
    5. The Trailhead prep trail for the exam

Which one first

  • New to Tableau: Desktop Foundations, then Data Analyst once LODs feel normal.
  • Analyst or developer: straight to Data Analyst if you have used Tableau daily for six months.
  • Admin: Server Administrator, then Architect.
  • Consultant: Data Analyst, then Consultant. Architect if you design platforms as well.

Every exam lists no formal prerequisite. The order above is advice, not a rule. My own credential so far is the Tableau Intermediate course credential from Salesforce, so I am walking this ladder with you.
