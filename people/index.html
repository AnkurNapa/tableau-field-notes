Tableau people to follow

I learnt most of my Tableau from people who wrote it down for free. These are the ones I keep going back to. One honest line each, links checked on 12 October 2026.

  • Andy Kriebel

    VizWiz

    Still publishing Tableau tutorials in 2026, from a full beginner course to when to use a highlight action over a filter. He co-hosted Makeover Monday with Eva Murray for years.

  • Ken and Kevin Flerlage

    The Flerlage Twins

    Long, careful blog posts that teach one technique properly, plus their Tiny Tableau Tips round-ups, now on round ten.

  • Jonathan Drummey

    Drawing with Numbers

    The deep end. Posts like his series on customising grand totals explain why Tableau behaves the way it does, not just which button to press.

  • Lindsey Poulter

    Portfolio

    A data visualisation designer and developer whose interactive pieces show how far design choices can carry a dashboard.

  • Tim Ngwena

    YouTube, @TableauTim

    High-quality video tutorials for analysts. The channel now covers the wider analytics market too, which helps when you need to see Tableau in context.

  • Michael Sandberg

    Data Visualization Blog

    A long-running blog that gathers data visualisation work and news from across the community in one place.

  • Adam Mico

    Medium

    Writes about the DataFam itself and runs the #DataFamRisingStars nominations, a good way to find newer voices.

  • Sarah Bartlett

    Sarah Loves Data

    Analytics and Tableau writing. Also the home of the #IronQuest community project.

  • Donna Coles

    Donna + DataViz

    Tableau Visionary and part of the Workout Wednesday leadership team. Her solution guides walk through each challenge step by step.

  • Tore Levinsen

    torelevinsen.com

    Tableau trainer and Visionary who posts a weekly #TinyTableauTip. Small, practical and easy to keep up with.

  • Ken Black

    Data Blends

    Worked examples of solving analytics problems with Tableau and Alteryx, often on large data sets.

  • Tristan Guillevin

    LaDataViz

    Builds tools around Tableau, such as Figma to Tableau and Viz Extensions. Free education sits alongside them.

  • Jim Dehner

    Tableau Newbies User Group

    Leads the Tableau Newbies User Group, where I co-lead with him. If you are just starting, his group is the gentlest door in.

For new names every month, read the DataFam Roundup on the Tableau blog and look through the Tableau Visionaries page.
