Start Tableau with the best free resources

Everything here is something I would send a new analyst on their first day. Every link was checked in October 2026. Each one says plainly what it is good for.

Install something today

You do not need a licence to start. Tableau Public is free and does almost everything Desktop does, with one catch: what you save there is public.

  • Tableau Public app Free

    Tableau

    The free desktop authoring app. Saves only to your Public profile, so never put work data in it.

  • Tableau Desktop trial Free trial

    Tableau

    A time-limited trial of the full product. Start it the week you can actually use it, not the week you sign up.

  • Tableau for Students Free to eligible

    Tableau

    Free Tableau for verified students, plus a student edition of Iron Viz. Check the current terms on the page.

  • Tableau for Teaching Free to eligible

    Tableau

    Free licences and course material for instructors teaching with Tableau.

  • Developer Program sandbox Free

    Tableau

    A free developer site for trying the REST, Embedding and Metadata APIs without touching production.

Official free training

Start here before paying for any course. The official material is better than its reputation.

  • Free training videos Free

    Tableau

    Short official videos on connecting, building and sharing. A sign-in may be needed for some.

  • Tour the Tableau Environment (Trailhead trail) Free

    Salesforce Trailhead

    About an hour on the workspace and the workflow. Good for day one.

  • Get Started with Data Visualization in Tableau Desktop (Trailhead trail) Free

    Salesforce Trailhead

    Under three hours on connecting, filtering, sorting and building. Badges if you like that sort of thing.

  • Tutorial: get started with Tableau Desktop Free

    Tableau Help

    A written, step by step first workbook with sample data. Still the best single first afternoon.

  • Tableau eLearning Paid

    Tableau

    Structured role-based learning paths, sold as a year of access with a free taster. Do the free material first.

  • Tableau Desktop and web authoring Help Free

    Tableau Help

    The reference you will use for years. Search it before you search anywhere else.

Practice data and challenges

Watching does not make you better. Building every week does.

  • Makeover Monday Free

    Makeover Monday

    One small data set and one chart to improve each week. The feedback sessions teach more than the data.

  • Workout Wednesday Free

    Workout Wednesday

    Rebuild a set viz exactly. Hard, humbling and the fastest route to real calculation skill.

  • Viz of the Day Free

    Tableau Public

    A daily pick from Tableau Public. Download one a week and work out how it was built.

  • Sample data Free

    Tableau Public

    Free data sets by topic, cleaner than real life, which is both the good and the bad news.

Books

Two are about Tableau. Three are about thinking clearly with charts, which matters more.

  • Storytelling with Data Paid

    Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic, Wiley, 2015

    Tool-free lessons on decluttering and focus. Read it before your first stakeholder dashboard.

  • The Big Book of Dashboards Paid

    Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer and Andy Cotgreave, Wiley, 2017

    Real dashboards with critiques, by scenario. The one to keep on the desk.

  • Visual Analytics with Tableau Paid

    Alexander Loth, Wiley, 2019

    A structured beginner-to-practitioner walk through Desktop. Some screens have aged; the method has not.

  • Communicating Data with Tableau Paid

    Ben Jones, O'Reilly, 2014

    Old version numbers, sound principles. Good on choosing the chart for the message.

  • Practical Tableau Paid

    Ryan Sleeper, O'Reilly, 2018

    A hundred tips and tutorials from a Tableau Visionary. Dip in rather than read cover to cover.

A 30-day starter plan

About forty minutes a day. Miss a day and carry on; do not restart.

  1. Week 1, Days 1 to 7

    Get your hands on it

    • Install Tableau Public and do the Help get-started tutorial
    • Learn blue against green pills and what Columns, Rows and Marks do
    • Build ten bar and line charts from sample data, one question each

  2. Week 2, Days 8 to 14

    Calculations and dates

    • Write IF, CASE and ZN calculations on your own data
    • Get continuous dates on an axis without help
    • Do your first Makeover Monday and post it

  3. Week 3, Days 15 to 21

    Dashboards people use

    • Build one dashboard with a filter action and a fixed size
    • Read the Which chart when page and redo one chart you got wrong
    • Watch one Newbie TUG recording and ask one question in the forums

  4. Week 4, Days 22 to 30

    Show your work

    • Try a Workout Wednesday, then compare with the solution
    • Learn one FIXED LOD and one table calc properly
    • Publish three vizzes on Public with a one-line description each

Then pick up the roadmap at level 1 and tick off what you already know.
